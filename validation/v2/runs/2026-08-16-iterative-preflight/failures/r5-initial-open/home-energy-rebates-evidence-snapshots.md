## E1 DOE Home Energy Rebates Program Page

Publisher: U.S. Department of Energy

URL: https://www.energy.gov/cmei/scep/home-energy-rebates-program

Kind: DOE program page

Observed facts used:

- HOMES offers rebates for eligible whole-home energy upgrade projects, with savings up to $8,000.
- HEEHR offers rebates for eligible appliances/building envelope measures, with household savings up to $14,000.
- Program status says rebates are available in select states and tells residents to contact state/territory offices.

## E5 DOE Program Notice 26-1

Publisher: U.S. Department of Energy

Effective date: 2026-05-29

URL: https://www.energy.gov/documents/program-notice-26-1

Kind: DOE program notice / guidance change

Observed facts used:

- Applies to HOMES grantees and gives guidance for programs already launched, not yet launched, or in conditional-award status.
- Launched programs must make changes within three months unless DOE approves extension.
- Non-launched programs must align with new requirements before launch.
- DOE removed several prior consumer-protection-plan elements and made some implementation plans optional.

## E6 DOE Program Notice 26-2

Publisher: U.S. Department of Energy

Effective date: 2026-05-29

URL: https://www.energy.gov/documents/program-notice-26-2

Kind: DOE program notice / guidance change

Observed facts used:

- Applies to HEEHR grantees and gives guidance for launched, non-launched, and conditional-award programs.
- Launched programs must change within three months; non-launched programs must align before launch.
- DOE removed fuel-switching allowances and required insulation/air sealing before heating/cooling upgrades unless already adequate.
- DOE encouraged point-of-sale, e-commerce, direct-to-consumer, marketplace, DIY, and mail-in pathways subject to constraints.
