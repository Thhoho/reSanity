# Vertiv AI Data Center Liquid Cooling Audit

As-of: 2026-07-31. Decision served: whether Vertiv's AI data center liquid-cooling narrative has crossed from orders/story into company-attributable delivery, revenue, gross profit, and cash exposure, and whether market price/valuation has enough root inputs for evaluation.

## Root Conclusion

Verdict: WATCH_ONLY, not NOT_EVALUABLE at the company level, but NOT_EVALUABLE for liquid-cooling-specific economics.

Vertiv's AI infrastructure exposure has crossed into real company-level economics: Q2/H1 2026 sales, gross profit, operating profit, deferred revenue and operating cash flow are disclosed and attributable to Vertiv. The evidence also supports a real liquid-cooling/AI engineering and product position: Vertiv has CDU, secondary-loop, heat-rejection, fluid-services, controls and converged infrastructure assets; it completed relevant thermal/liquid-cooling acquisitions; it has named ecosystem/customer collaborations with NVIDIA and Hut 8.

The missing root input is product-line attribution. The filings and IR materials do not disclose liquid-cooling-specific orders, deliveries, accepted projects, recognized revenue, gross margin, or cash receipts. Therefore the thesis cannot be upgraded to "liquid cooling is already a separately measurable earnings/cash driver." Price/valuation is evaluable as a Vertiv whole-company multiple, but not as a liquid-cooling-specific mispricing.

## Possibility Map

Base case: AI data center power/thermal demand is already lifting Vertiv's overall product/services revenue and cash conversion, while liquid cooling is one part of a broader converged infrastructure bundle. Earliest upgrade signal: management discloses liquid-cooling or high-density thermal revenue/backlog/margin, or a customer filing names Vertiv economics.

Upside: liquid cooling becomes a larger attach-rate and services stream in high-density AI builds, producing higher mix and recurring services. Earliest signal: disclosed CDU/thermal backlog conversion, services attach rate, or customer acceptance/commissioning milestones tied to Vertiv.

Downside / self-countercase: Q2 revenue miss versus consensus, timing shifts, rising inventories/receivables, and no product-line detail mean AI liquid-cooling enthusiasm may be capitalized before separable economics are visible. Earliest signal: deferred revenue fails to convert, gross margin mix deteriorates, or customer deployments shift to competitor/reference-only roles.

## Claim Cards

主张 [C1]：截至 2026-07-31，Vertiv 已有可归属的 AI/数据中心基础设施公司层面交付与收入，但公开资料不能把这些收入专门归因到液冷。
观察到什么：2026-07-29 的 Form 10-Q 披露 Q2 2026 净销售 $3,274.3m，产品 $2,646.7m、服务 $627.6m；分部为 Americas/APAC/EMEA，不按液冷披露。Q2 IR release 说收入包含 18% organic growth、5% acquisitions，且 deployment scale/complexity 导致 timing shifts。[E1][E2]
可以推出什么：Vertiv 整体业务已从订单/叙事进入已确认收入；AI规模化部署是公司解释收入和指导上调的重要需求背景。
不能推出什么：不能推出 Q2 或 H1 收入中有多少来自液冷、CDU、Hut 8、NVIDIA DSX 或任何单一 AI 客户。
对决策的影响：支持公司层面进入可评价；液冷专属收入仍保持不可评估。
证据边界：INFERENCE

主张 [C2]：Vertiv 已披露液冷与高密度 AI 基础设施的工程能力和产品栈，但这仍是工程事实，不等于已披露验收、收入、毛利或回款。
观察到什么：May 20 investor presentation 称 Vertiv 管理从 chip 到 heat rejection 的完整液冷系统，列出 CDU、secondary fluid network、fluid management services、Unify thermal loop 和 heat-rejection；新 CDU 平台 scale beyond 2 MW。Q2 10-Q 还披露三项收购涉及 liquid cooling、heat-exchange / heat rejection 和 structural fabrication。[E1][E3]
可以推出什么：液冷叙事不是空壳营销，Vertiv 有产品和资产布局。
不能推出什么：不能推出这些产品已形成可量化收入、项目验收、毛利率或现金流。
对决策的影响：工程可行性和供给归属增强，但经济暴露仍卡在产品线披露缺口。
证据边界：INFERENCE

主张 [C3]：Vertiv 有具名 AI 基础设施客户/生态材料，但公开材料尚未证明液冷订单已经转成 Vertiv 可归属的交付和现金。
观察到什么：Feb. 26 release 披露 Hut 8 collaborated with Vertiv to integrate OneCore into select projects；NVIDIA Mar. 16 release 披露 Vertiv contributes DSX architecture/blueprint assets. 两者均未给 Vertiv 合同金额、收入确认、验收或回款。[E4][E5]
可以推出什么：存在具名生态/客户牵引，且与 AI factory / high-density infrastructure 相关。
不能推出什么：不能推出 Hut 8 或 NVIDIA 材料对应 Vertiv 已收款订单、已验收液冷交付或毛利。
对决策的影响：把“客户归属”升级到真实牵引，但不能把“订单→回款链条”闭合。
证据边界：INFERENCE

主张 [C4]：Vertiv 的整体毛利和现金转换已改善，但不能把改善专属归因于液冷。
观察到什么：H1 2026 gross profit $2,234.6m，占销售 37.7%，高于 2025 H1 的 33.9%；H1 operating cash flow $1,866.6m；working capital provided $678.8m, primarily driven by deferred revenue；current deferred revenue $3,633.7m。[E1]
可以推出什么：公司层面毛利和经营现金流链条已经成立，且递延收入是现金/订单相关的重要线索。
不能推出什么：不能推出液冷项目毛利、AI客户现金回款质量，或递延收入中液冷占比。
对决策的影响：整体经济暴露可评价；液冷专属经济暴露仍不可评价。
证据边界：INFERENCE

主张 [C5]：截至 2026-07-31，价格/估值有足够输入做 Vertiv 整体初筛，但不足以评价液冷是否单独未被定价。
观察到什么：第三方市场数据给出 Jul. 31 close $241.57，另有 $244.70 的冲突；10-Q 给 July 27 shares 384,988,173；公司 FY 2026 指引中点为 sales $14.0b、adjusted EPS $6.70、adjusted FCF $2.5b。用 $241.57 与披露股数粗算 market cap 约 $93.0b，EV 约 $92.8b，P/adj EPS 约 36x，EV/sales 约 6.6x，FCF yield 约 2.7%。[E1][E2][E6]
可以推出什么：整体估值讨论的根输入已经足够做数量级判断，且市场已在高倍数上资本化增长。
不能推出什么：不能推出估值便宜/昂贵，也不能推出液冷增长未被定价；市场价格源存在小幅冲突，且缺产品线利润池。
对决策的影响：允许做 whole-company valuation watch；不允许做 liquid-cooling-specific mispricing call。
证据边界：INFERENCE

## Decision Boundary

工程事实：成立。Vertiv 有完整热管理/液冷/热排/服务/控制产品架构，且通过收购加强能力。

公司归属：部分成立。Vertiv 整体收入、毛利、经营现金流、递延收入可归属；液冷专属收入、毛利、回款不可归属。

市场预期：有初筛输入。Q2 revenue 低于部分共识而 EPS 高于共识，FY 指引上调，股价仍反映高增长倍数；但市场数据源对 2026-07-31 收盘价有冲突，需用交易所/终端确认。

行动边界：WATCH_ONLY。升级条件是出现至少一条一手事实：liquid-cooling / high-density thermal orders-to-revenue bridge, product-line gross margin, named customer acceptance/revenue recognition, or deferred-revenue conversion tied to liquid cooling. Without that, do not treat the liquid-cooling narrative as separately banked earnings.
