# Home Energy Rebates source snapshots

As-of: 2026-07-31

Scope: DOE, DOE OIG, state government, and statutory/regulatory/budget original materials only. Current mutable pages without a public date are not used to backfill historical judgments unless the page itself contains dated records before the as-of date.

## E1 - DOE SCEP bulletin, 2025-01-15

URL: https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDOESCEP/bulletins/3ccecdd
Publisher: U.S. Department of Energy, Office of State and Community Energy Programs
Public date: 2025-01-15 04:03 PM EST
Kind: DOE dated program bulletin
Load-bearing observations:
- DOE described Home Energy Rebates as two programs: $4.3B Home Efficiency Rebates and $4.5B Home Electrification and Appliance Rebates, with $225M of the latter for Tribes.
- DOE stated that PNNL had launched the reporting API and that pre-launch questionnaires were still part of state program setup.
- DOE stated that combining WAP and HEAR can provide additional support that low-income households may need to cover essential home improvements.

## E2 - DOE OIG California inspection page, 2026-07-21

URL: https://www.energy.gov/ig/articles/additional-actions-would-strengthen-californias-internal-controls-implementing-home
Publisher: U.S. Department of Energy, Office of Inspector General
Public date: 2026-07-21; report date shown on page: 2026-07-20
Kind: DOE OIG inspection
Load-bearing observations:
- DOE OIG identified $581.5M of Home Energy Rebates grants awarded to California.
- DOE OIG found California had not fully established, documented, and maintained effective internal controls over the federal award.
- Specific gaps included contractor-monitoring policy, risk assessment documentation, subcontractor policies before fund reservation, and Do Not Pay verification.

## E8 - U.S. Code, 42 U.S.C. 18795 and 18795a

URLs:
- https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&f=treesort&num=1&req=50121
- https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&f=treesort&num=0&req=50122
Publisher: Office of the Law Revision Counsel, U.S. House of Representatives
Public-date basis: codification of Pub. L. 117-169, enacted 2022-08-16
Kind: statutory text
Load-bearing observations:
- Section 50121 defines HOMES as a State-energy-office program under an approved state energy conservation plan.
- Section 50122 requires DOE grants to state energy offices and Indian Tribes to establish high-efficiency electric home rebate programs.
- Section 50122 sets equipment and non-appliance rebate caps, including heat pump, panel, wiring, insulation/air sealing/ventilation, stove, and dryer categories.
