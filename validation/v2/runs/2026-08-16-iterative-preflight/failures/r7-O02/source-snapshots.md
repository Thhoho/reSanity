## S1 - 42 U.S.C. 18795 / 18795a

URL: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&path=%2Fprelim%40title42%2Fchapter162

Public status: U.S. Code page; search result stated text in effect on June 2, 2026 for the section-specific page.

Key observations:
- Section 18795 appropriates $4.3 billion for HOMES grants to State energy offices, available through September 30, 2031.
- Section 18795a appropriates $4.275 billion for state HEEHR grants and $225 million for Tribes, available through September 30, 2031.
- HOMES requires state applications/plans and rebate completion by September 30, 2031.
- HEEHR requires income verification and point-of-sale guidelines; appliance and non-appliance caps include heat pumps, heat pump water heaters, panels, wiring, insulation/air sealing/ventilation, dryers, stoves/cooktops/ranges/ovens.

## S6 - DOE Program Notice 26-1 HOMES

URL: https://www.energy.gov/documents/program-notice-26-1

Effective date: May 29, 2026.

Key observations:
- Applies to already launched, not-yet-launched, and conditional-award HOMES grantees.
- Launched programs must make changes within three months unless DOE approves extension.
- Not-yet-launched programs must align before launch.
- DOE allowed shipping/travel costs for territories, Alaska, and Hawaii where products are not locally available.
- DOE identifies multifamily implementation as especially challenging.
- DOE added DNP database vetting for contractors and changed several consumer protection/reporting requirements.

## S7 - DOE Program Notice 26-2 HEEHR

URL: https://www.energy.gov/documents/program-notice-26-2

Effective date: May 29, 2026.

Key observations:
- Applies to already launched, not-yet-launched, and conditional-award HEEHR grantees.
- Launched programs must make changes within three months; not-yet-launched programs must align before launch.
- DOE removed fuel-switching allowances, required insulation/air sealing before heating/cooling upgrades unless already adequate, and allowed shipping/travel costs for territories, Alaska, and Hawaii where products are not locally available.
- DOE required DNP contractor vetting.

## S12 - DOE OIG California inspection

URL: https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2026-07/DOE-OIG-26-42.pdf

Publication date: July 20, 2026; DOE article date July 21, 2026.

Key observations:
- California had $581.5 million in grants awarded/allocated under DOE oversight.
- California launched HEEHR in October 2024; at OIG field work time, HOMES had not launched.
- OIG found California had not fully established, documented, and maintained effective internal controls.
- Specific issues included lack of monitoring policy, undocumented risk assessment, inadequate multifamily policies before reservations, and non-use of Treasury Do Not Pay for eligibility vetting.
- OIG noted review of 10 out of more than 1,000 rebate packages per quarter and three multifamily reservation approvals totaling $4,451,250.
